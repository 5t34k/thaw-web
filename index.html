thawfrost backup recovery

Turn your frozen shares back into a spendable key.

Reconstruct a Bitcoin wallet xpriv and descriptor from your Frostsnap backup shares. For emergency recovery when the app isn't available.

This is not an official Frostsnap product. It's an independent, community-built web version of the official recovery script, frostsnap-thaw - source at github.com/frostsnap/frostsnap-thaw. Only use in an emergency on an offline device.

Emergency use only - run air-gapped

This tool reconstructs your full secret key and shows it on screen - the exact situation FROST exists to prevent. Only use it on a fresh, offline machine you trust, and wipe that machine when you're done. Anyone who sees this screen can take your funds.

  1. 1

    Enter your shares

    One share per box, formatted #<index> <25 words>. Add exactly your threshold number of shares - e.g. 2 for a 2-of-3 wallet. Each is checksum-verified as you type.

  2. 2

    Choose the network

    Mainnet for real bitcoin. Testnet produces a tprv for testing.

  3. 3

    Recover your wallet

    Combines shares via Lagrange interpolation, verifies the polynomial checksum (confirming they belong to one wallet), and derives your keys.

    Wallet recovered

    Import the descriptor below into any descriptor-aware wallet to access your funds.

    How to import into a wallet
    1. The descriptor is the complete recovery artifact - it encodes the key, the Taproot script type, and the derivation path, and carries its own checksum. Import it into any descriptor-aware wallet.
    2. Bitcoin Core must be v28.0 or newer. Earlier versions reject the <0;1> multipath descriptor (BIP 389).
    3. Rescan from before the wallet was created. Use a rescan timestamp of 0 (genesis) or 1735689600 (2025-01-01 UTC). A now timestamp scans nothing prior and silently finds no coins.
